Rodillian Runners is a small, friendly running club based in Stanley, Wakefield. We meet for training most Tuesdays at 7:00pm at Stanley Rodillians RUFC, Lee Moor Road, Stanley, WF3 4EF. Once a month we have the [Distance League](/club-activities/distance-league/) event where we meet at a variety of locations shown in the [club calendar](/club-activities/).

If you are new to running or just want to run with a group of friendly, like-minded people then get in touch. We're a sociable bunch and love to meet new people!

We are a small club, so we don't have separate groups. We all run and train together, and our club ethos is "no runner is ever left behind." Whether you want to run a sub-20 5k, complete your first marathon or just be part of a running community, we can offer you something.

So, if you are 18 or over, would like a bit more variety in your training and want to make some new friends, [contact us](/contact/) or check the [calendar](/club-activities/) to see where we are and turn up. We're happy for any potential new member to attend training free of charge for the first 3 or 4 weeks. There's no obligation to become a member, but we really hope that you'll enjoy it and decide to officially join our club.
