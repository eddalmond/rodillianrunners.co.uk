No Runner Left Behind

Whether you want to run a sub-20 5k, complete your first marathon, or just be part of a running community, we can offer you something. Our club ethos is simple: we all run and train together, and no runner is ever left behind on the road.

We're a sociable bunch and love meeting new people. If you're 18 or over and want a bit more variety in your training — come along.
